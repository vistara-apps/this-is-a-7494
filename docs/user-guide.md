## Getting Started

SolVote is a web application that allows communities to create and vote on proposals using the Solana blockchain. All votes are recorded on-chain, ensuring transparency and verifiability.

### System Requirements

- A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- A Phantom wallet or other Solana wallet
- SOL for transaction fees (very minimal amounts needed)

## Connecting Your Wallet

1. Visit the SolVote website.
2. Click the "Connect Phantom" button in the top-right corner.
3. Approve the connection request in your Phantom wallet.
4. Once connected, you'll see your wallet address displayed in the navigation bar.

**Note**: If you don't have a Phantom wallet, you can install it from [phantom.app](https://phantom.app/).

## Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides an overview of your voting activity and community proposals:

- **Total Proposals**: The number of proposals in your community
- **Active Proposals**: Proposals that are currently open for voting
- **Total Votes**: The total number of votes cast across all proposals
- **Your Votes**: The number of proposals you've voted on

The dashboard also displays recent proposals and voting activity charts.

## Viewing Proposals

1. Click on "Proposals" in the navigation menu.
2. You'll see a list of all proposals, which you can filter by:
   - All Proposals
   - Active (currently open for voting)
   - Completed (voting has ended)
   - My Votes (proposals you've voted on)

3. Click on any proposal to view its details, including:
   - Title and description
   - Creator
   - Voting progress (yes/no percentages)
   - Time remaining for voting
   - Your vote (if you've voted)

## Voting on Proposals

1. Navigate to the "Proposals" page.
2. Find an active proposal you want to vote on.
3. Click the "Yes" or "No" button to cast your vote.
4. Confirm the transaction in your Phantom wallet.
5. Once confirmed, your vote will be recorded on the blockchain.

**Note**: You can only vote once on each proposal, and you cannot change your vote after it's submitted.

## Creating Proposals

1. Click on "Create Proposal" in the navigation menu.
2. Fill out the proposal form:
   - **Title**: A clear, concise title for your proposal
   - **Description**: Detailed information about what you're proposing
   - **Voting Duration**: How long the voting period should last

3. Click "Create Proposal" to submit.
4. Confirm the transaction in your Phantom wallet.
5. Once confirmed, your proposal will be created and open for voting.

**Tips for effective proposals**:
- Be clear and specific about what you're proposing
- Explain the benefits to the community
- Consider potential risks or drawbacks
- Keep the title concise but descriptive

## Managing Communities

### Creating a Community

1. If you're not part of a community yet, you'll see an option to create one.
2. Click "Create Community" and provide a name for your community.
3. Confirm the transaction in your Phantom wallet.
4. Once confirmed, you'll be the admin of the new community.

### Community Administration

As a community admin, you can:
- Manage community settings
- Create proposals
- View all community activity

## FAQ

### How much does it cost to use SolVote?

SolVote requires small amounts of SOL for transaction fees:
- Creating a community: ~0.01 SOL
- Creating a proposal: ~0.005 SOL
- Voting: ~0.0001 SOL

### Is my vote anonymous?

No, votes are recorded on the Solana blockchain with your wallet address. Anyone can see how you voted by examining the blockchain.

### Can I change my vote?

No, once a vote is recorded on the blockchain, it cannot be changed.

### What happens when a proposal ends?

When a proposal's voting period ends, no more votes can be cast. The final result is permanently recorded on the blockchain.

### How can I verify the results?

All voting data is stored on the Solana blockchain. You can verify results by:
1. Viewing the proposal details in the SolVote app
2. Using a Solana explorer to examine the on-chain data
3. Running your own analysis of the blockchain data

### Can I use SolVote for my organization?

Yes! SolVote is designed for any community that needs transparent governance. Contact us for enterprise options with additional features.
